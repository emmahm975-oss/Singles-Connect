Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration really free?

Yes! Creating your profile and viewing matches is completely free. You only pay the small connection fee when you are ready to message someone.

How do I pay from my country?

Our system automatically detects your country and converts the KES 899 fee into your local currency (e.g., UGX, TZS, NGN). The admin will provide specific payment instructions.

Is my information safe?

Absolutely. We protect your data and your photos are only visible to registered members. You can block or report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
